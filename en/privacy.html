Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Introduction

Mira is a Windows desktop AI voice assistant developed by Mojosoft. This policy explains how Mira handles your data.

Data Collection

Mira does not collect or transmit your personal information. All data is stored locally on your PC. No data is sent to Mojosoft's servers.

Locally Stored Data

Mira stores the following data locally on your PC (%LOCALAPPDATA%\Mira\):

  • settings.json — App settings (AI provider, TTS, wake word, etc.)
  • secrets.dat — API keys (encrypted with Windows DPAPI)
  • memory.json — Information Mira remembers from conversations
  • schedules.json — Schedules and alarms
  • diag.log — Diagnostic logs (auto-rotated)

Data Sent to AI Providers

When using cloud AI providers (Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic, etc.), transcribed text from your voice input, together with what the AI uses to answer (the memory, schedule and notes Mira keeps, and tool results such as web search results or clipboard text read in Live mode), is sent to the selected provider's API. Each provider's privacy policy applies.

Note on the Gemini API free tier: According to Google's pricing page, on the free tier the content you send (such as conversation text, the memory, schedule and notes given to the AI, tool results such as clipboard text, Screen Vision screenshots, and Gemini Live audio and screen captures) may be used by Google to improve its products. It is not used when your API key belongs to a project with billing enabled (paid tier). See the Gemini API pricing page for the current terms.

When using local AI (Ollama, LM Studio, Lemonade), no data is sent externally.

Screen Vision

When using Screen Vision, screenshot images are sent to the selected Vision-capable AI provider. Using a local Vision model (e.g., Ollama + llava) keeps all data on your PC (except for screen captures during Gemini Live mode, which are sent to Google regardless of this setting; see below).

Gemini Live Mode

In Gemini Live mode, audio data is streamed in real-time to Google's servers. Google's terms of service and privacy policy apply. Live mode is off by default. When enabled in Settings, it starts when the wake phrase is detected ("Mira live" by default) and ends with a stop phrase. Because surrounding sounds can occasionally be mistaken for the wake phrase, from v2.3.1 Live starts only when the phrase is detected with higher confidence than a regular wake word. If you don't use Live, we recommend leaving it disabled.

When Live mode's Screen Capture option is on, any time the AI looks at your screen the screenshot is also sent to Google (from v2.3.1 it is off by default for new installs; if you installed an earlier version, your previous setting is kept). From v2.3.1 the AI is instructed to look at the screen only when you ask, but as this relies on the AI's judgment, it may still occasionally look without being asked. If you don't want your screen sent, turn off Screen Capture in the Live mode tab of Settings.

Microphone Usage

Mira monitors microphone input for wake word detection, but audio data is neither saved nor transmitted (except during Gemini Live mode; see above). Speech recognition (STT) is processed by a local Whisper model on your PC, and audio data is discarded after transcription.
